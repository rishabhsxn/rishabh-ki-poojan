A calmer lit palace

After the tap, the start page fills up: a marigold garland, fireworks that never stop, lamps scattered over the water and a bright ghat in the corner. Below is what is busy today, then four ways to calm it, each on a phone and on a computer. The previews run the site's own drawing code, so they light up just as the site does. Tap one to light it again, or press Replay.

Today

What makes it busy

  1. The garland crosses the top and, on a phone, hangs down both sides of the names, boxing them in.
  2. Fireworks burst around and behind the names, every two or three seconds, for as long as the page stays open.
  3. Eleven lamps on the water, placed at random, each with a wide glow. Together they read as haze, and one sits right under "Scroll down".
  4. The ghat is four flat orange ovals with 18 lamps on a phone (25 on a computer). It is the brightest thing on screen, so the eye goes to the corner instead of the palace. It doesn't read as steps, and the music button sits on top of it.
  5. Every light is equally bright: windows, reflection, lamps, ghat, garland and fireworks all compete, so nothing leads to the names and the palace. In all, 29 lamps on a phone and 36 on a computer.

The palace itself, the names and the way the light climbs are good, and every option keeps them. The changes are all around the edges.

In every option

  • Fireworks: one volley of five as the light climbs, bursting between the names and the domes, then a still sky. The switch above brings back today's endless fireworks if you miss them.
  • A smaller glow round each lamp, about a quarter smaller, so they read as flames rather than haze.
  • Lamps on the water placed by hand, with clear water round "Scroll down" and the music button.
  • Nothing hanging beside the names. Where there is a garland, it stays along the top edge.

Ghat

For: even and orderly, and it gives the screen a floor to stand on.

Against: it hides the lower part of the palace's reflection, and on a computer the steps are a thin band, more frame than feature.

When scrolling: the steps sink out of view as the camera moves off across the water.

My pick

Keep the ghat on the right, but build it properly: real steps rising from the water to the bank, with a small chhatri at the top.

Ghat
Five stone steps drawn in the scene's own perspective. The stone stays dark like the night, and only the step edges catch the light. A row of lamps runs along every other step, pointing towards the palace. The chhatri has one lamp inside.

For: it keeps a ghat, which belongs on Lake Pichola, and now it reads as steps. The rows of lamps lead the eye to the palace instead of pulling it into the corner.

Against: the most detailed of the four, so it is the most work to carry through the scroll. On a phone it fills the bottom-right corner under the music button.

What I need from you

  • One of A, B, C or D. Mixing is fine too, for example A's water with B's thin garland.
  • The garland: none, a thin swag, or as today.
  • The fireworks: one volley, or keep going.
  • Anything to change in the one you pick, such as fewer lamps on the steps, no chhatri, or a different arch.

Then I'll build it into the site, check it on a phone and a computer, including the scroll into the palace, and save it as the next version. It goes live when you push.
