Tapping a photo, in every option

The photo grows out of its frame to fill the screen, and goes back into its frame when you close it.

  1. Opening. A tap grows the photo from its frame to full screen over a dark ground. The frame's arch flattens into a plain edge as it grows. A counter shows which photo it is.
  2. Next photo. Swipe sideways, or use the arrow keys on a computer.
  3. Closing. Swipe down, tap ×, or press Esc. The photo shrinks back into the frame it belongs to.

The song and the mute button carry on as they are.

My pick: P2, the jharokha window

It shows all ten photos large and in order, one at a time, inside a Rajasthani frame, and its motion runs the same way as your scroll. It is calm, which suits your "not cluttered", and the shutters give it one memorable moment without another tap-to-start.

If you would rather keep the page shorter, P1 does the same job in one screen, but guests have to swipe. Options can also be mixed, for example P2's window ending in P5's spread-out grid so guests get a last look at all ten together.

What I need from you

  • The option you want (or a mix), and anything you would change in it.
  • The photos at full size. Portrait photos suit the arch options (P1, P2, P4, P6), since the arch covers the top corners. Landscape photos are fine in P3 and P5.
  • The order, and which photo should come first.
  • A short caption for each, if you want them: a place, a date or a line. They are optional.
  • Anything you would rather not have public. The site is open to anyone with the link.

I will resize each photo to about 1600 px on the long side and save it as WebP, around 200 KB each. They load only when the section comes near, so the palace at the start opens as quickly as it does now.
